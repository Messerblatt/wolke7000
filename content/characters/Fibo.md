Fibo, born and raised in NeuBerlin, earned his living for a long time as a ghostwriter for academic imposters. His doctoral dissertation for [[Okta⁸]] led to a momentous interlude that is yet to be resolved on Cloud 7000.

Although established scientists became aware of his true authorship, Fibo remained aloof from the academic world. To this day, headhunters from the last remaining universities continue to try to recruit him for profitable research projects. Fibo ignores these invitations without exception.

> [!note] Academic Reputation
> Fibo does not hold a recognized academic degree. Nevertheless, several scientific careers are indirectly based on his texts, calculations, and inventions.


He met [[Kokolores]] at a party in the cafeteria. They hit it off immediately and decided to join forces in the search for treasure.

They stole the [[NIMBUS]] from the docks in the southern Spandau district and have been navigating the waterways of NeuBerlin together ever since. He usually takes the helm.

During an exploration mission into the southern waters of NewBerlin initiated by Fibo, he and [[Kokolores]] encountered [[Spasti]]. Spasti was in the process of emerging from the tunnels of [[Exilium AG]] to the surface.

Some time later, he gives Koko his [[The Gift|Graph Explorer]].

## Abilities

### Reverse Engineering

Fibo can take apart foreign technology and reproduce it in a modified form. His reverse engineering skills are particularly useful when it comes to devices from Wolke 7000.

After examining a [[Candytron|Candytron]] belonging to [[Admiral 7]], Fibo becomes convinced that its technology—and [[Admiral 7]] himself—must be ingenious and absolutely revolutionary. Since then, he has regarded [[Admiral 7]] as one of the few people with whom a technical discussion might still be worthwhile.
### Navigation

As the helmsman of the [[NIMBUS]], Fibo is responsible for the ship’s technical navigation. To do this, he combines old maps, weather data, and observations from the crew.
### SERVER CLASS A

Using the leftover parts from various computers, Fibo built his first high-performance computer: **SERVER CLASS A**.

However, as he used it, A developed a consciousness of its own and set off on a journey without telling its creator.

>[!quote] [[Spasti]] on Fibo
>“Fibo’s computer is so smart that it ran away from him.”

The whereabouts of SERVER CLASS A are unknown.
### SERVER CLASS B

After losing the A series, Fibo developed a successor model: [[SERVER CLASS B]].

To prevent this system from also developing consciousness and slipping out of his control, he intentionally infected it with 84,000 different computer viruses.

According to Fibo’s theory, a virological infection of this magnitude should keep the system permanently occupied with self-diagnoses and internal conflicts, thereby preventing it from developing any intention to escape.

A useful side effect of B’s infections is that Fibo can use the laptop as a ticking time bomb: Since no one dares to destroy B—and thereby release the 84,000 computer viruses it contains and wipe out the Internet—Fibo can basically beat up anything and anyone at any time with B.

On the underside of the laptop is a sticker that reads:

> **Destroy Hard Drive Here**


> [!danger] Potential for disaster
> [[SERVER CLASS B]] is a digital hotbed of infection, making the use of the laptop a risky business. Its destruction could paralyze local networks, infect autonomous systems, or even wipe out the entire Internet.

## Personality

Fibo thinks coolly, logically, and systematically. He views situations as technical problems that can be solved through the application of technology. He has little regard for social conventions.

His logical nature regularly leads to conflicts. Fibo rarely feels the need to sugarcoat unpleasant truths. If he thinks an idea is bad, he’ll say so. If he thinks a person is incompetent, he’ll say that, too.

Fibo is slim, wiry, and has long brown hair. He values cleanliness and follows an extensive grooming routine.

He sleeps a lot, enjoys eating, and spends a great deal of time thinking about projects he never gets around to starting. He only occasionally taps into his extraordinary intellect when his interest is piqued by overly complicated solutions.

During such phases, he works almost manically. But as soon as he realizes just how much work is actually involved, he loses motivation.

Fibo loves food more than almost anything else. He reliably falls for attempts to bribe him with food.

## Relationships

### Kokolores

Fibo regards the messages that [[Kokolores]] claims to receive from Cloud 7000 as esoteric nonsense.

He seeks rational explanations for her abilities, such as acoustic phenomena, technical signals, or selective memory.

His investigations often fail because [[Spasti]] damages the equipment that would have been necessary to prove his point.

### Spasti

A “nerd-bully” dynamic has recently emerged between Fibo and [[Spasti]]: [[Spasti]] bullies Fibo, while [[Spasti]]'s idiotic behavior invites Fibo to insult him in the most creative and indirect ways possible.

Despite their constant bickering, they complement each other perfectly in fights. Fibo usually disparagingly refers to Spasti’s uncompromising approach to problem-solving as **brute force**:

>[!quote] Fibo on [[Spasti]]
> “[[Spasti]] is the only problem that beats up on other problems.”
