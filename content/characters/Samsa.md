>[!abstract] Profile
>[[Samsa]] ([[Open Wings]] ID **OW-Samsa-071**) is the carrier pigeon of the [[NIMBUS]]. She transports letters and small items for the crew, serving as their personal postal service. She shares a particularly close bond with Koko, who is her most trusted confidante and best friend.
