## NeuBerlin als Wasserstadt

...

Wolke7000 is a solarpunk/metafictional future-Berlin world
where information, infrastructure, ecology, and human relationships
matter more than military power.

The tone combines:
- tenderness
- absurd bureaucracy
- technical improvisation
- melancholy
- strange humor
- ruined urban grandeur

---

## The Core Experience Pillars

1. Explore a living flooded city.
2. Build relationships rather than conquer.
3. Learn through objects, places, and people.
4. Trade and move through imperfect information.
5. Let the world remember the player.
6. Occasionally let the fiction break through the interface.
