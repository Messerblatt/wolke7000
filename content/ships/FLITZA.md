FLITZA is the best-known, though not the only, vehicle in the Shark series. [[funky (dot) blend|Funky]] developed several related designs for various purposes.

| Shark Model | Function              | Special Feature                                        |
| ----------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Sprint**  | Racing boat           | Maximum acceleration over short distances              |
| **Courier** | Courier services      | Small cargo box and open-wing receiving compartment    |
| **Rescue**  | Water rescue          | Side-mounted retrieval mechanism for injured persons   |
| **Bite**    | Interceptor boat      | Reinforced bow and towing hook                         |
| **FLITZA**  | Personal prototype    | Unreasonably fast; officially "still under testing"    |

The series was never patented. All blueprints are publicly available; however, the FLITZA version includes several modules that [[funky (dot) blend|funky]] has labeled as **"use at your own stupidity."**
