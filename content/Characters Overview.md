 
## [[Kokolores]]

![[imgs/characters/Kokolores/koko_third.png]]


> [!abstract] Profile
> [[Kokolores]], usually called **Koko**, is an angel, a treasure hunter, and the initiator of the [[Suicide Squad]]. She is among the earliest followers of "Cloud 7000" (Wolke 7000) and is regarded as its spiritual leader aboard the [[NIMBUS]].

## [[Fibo]]

![[fibo_third.jpg]]


> [!abstract] Profile
> [[Fibo]] is the helmsman of the [[NIMBUS]], as well as a hacker, inventor, and all-around technical genius. His extensive expertise in everything related to technology makes him indispensable to the [[Suicide Squad]].

---
## [[Spasti]]


> [!abstract] Profile
> [[Spasti]] is a troublemaker, anarchist, arsonist, and close-quarters combatant for [[NIMBUS]]. He drinks fuel, can breathe fire, and has a knack for abruptly ending political debates with well-placed blows. How his body withstands such strain remains a scientific mystery. [[Fibo]] describes him as a “biochemical impossibility,” a “walking engine failure,” or “proof that reason does not exist.”

---

## [[Samsa]]

![[imgs/characters/Samsa/samsa_third.png]]

>[!abstract] Profile
>[[Samsa]] ([[Open Wings]] ID **OW-Samsa-071**) is the carrier pigeon of the [[NIMBUS]]. She transports letters and small items for the crew, serving as their personal postal service. She shares a particularly close bond with Koko, who is her most trusted confidante and best friend.

---
## [[Nero]]


![[imgs/characters/Nero/nero_third.png]]

>[!abstract] Profile
>[[Nero]] is presumably the mastermind behind [[Exilium AG]]. His habit of lying with pathological intensity, combined with his tendency to appear and vanish with startling suddenness, makes him difficult to read. Virtually nothing is known about him—a fact that brings him great amusement. [[Fibo]] refers to him as a **"404."** Whenever Nero appears, [[Spasti]] falls into a state of peculiar trauma, likely linked to his own past within Exilium.

## [[Mia Von Schöneberg]]

![[imgs/characters/MiaVonSchöneberg/mia_3_third.png]]

> [!abstract] Profile
> [[Mia Von Schöneberg]] is an exile, a socialite, and a minister heading a government department—the very name of which reveals that [[Neu Berlin]] long ago ceased to feel any shame regarding sensible administrative structures. Following the theft of the nobility’s crown, she was banished for life to Neu Berlin, where she took charge of the **Ministry of Chive Reform**. Her rooftop raves are considered mandatory social engagements for anyone who is important, wishes to appear important, or at the very least owns a peacock.


---

## [[Gojan]]

> [!abstract] Profile
> [[Gojan]] is a tsunami-catcher, the owner of the New Berlin Waterworks, and one of the three "epiphanies" of the saga. Declared a hero at the age of just 27, he is a rebel worn down by the generational war. While [[Admiral 7]] elevates posterity to the role of judge over the present, Gojan has transformed the Admiral’s "millennial imperative" into a concrete life mission: he seeks to build a future that no longer has to bow to the past.


---


## [[Solo Mio]]

![[imgs/characters/SoloMio/solo.png]]

> [!abstract] Profile
> [[Solo Mio]] is the captain of the [[EVERYTHING|EVERYTHING]], a wholesaler, and a harpoon hunter. Having played a key role in the founding of New Berlin, he knows the city like the back of his hand. He has a knack for smuggling contraband past customs—and he’s a terrible singer. On top of that, he’s a millionaire.

## [[Salt Brah]]

![[salt_brah_third.png]]

> [!abstract] Profile
> [[Salt Brah]] is the founder of the [[Open Wings]] Foundation and captain of the [[INFINITY]]. Born and raised during the Alster War in Hamburg_2, he first gained public attention through his illegal concerts and mass uprisings. In Neu Berlin, he—alongside [[Ciao Von Bella]] and [[funky (dot) blend|Funky.blend]]—serves as one of the three administrators of the [[Tempelhof Diskoord]].
