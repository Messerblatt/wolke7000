The canteen is closed.

A handwritten sign hangs on the door:

> “No soup today.”

There is a small [[brass slot]] underneath.

:::requires-item{item="open-wing-receipt"}

The brass slot clicks.

A narrow strip of paper falls out.

> Delivery failed.
> Recipient: Canteen Kreuzberg
> Sender: UNKNOWN
> Package transferred to EVERYTHING storage.

The receipt contains a storage number:

**E-4417**

:::

:::requires-item{item="rusty-soup-ladle"}

The door is still locked.

But the ladle fits perfectly into the brass slot.

This raises several questions, most of them about whoever designed the lock.

```item
turn it
```

:::requires-item{item="turn-it"}

Something unlocks inside.

## Inside

The canteen smells faintly of onions, machine oil and old rain.

There are twelve tables. Eleven chairs. And one chair hanging from the ceiling.

:::



:::requires-item{item="rusty-soup-ladle"}
## The wall
On the far wall hangs a faded photograph. Most of the faces have been scratched out.
:::



:::requires-item{item="pfauenfeder"}
One detail suddenly looks familiar.
A peacock feather is pinned to the coat of one of the people in the photograph. Deliberately!
Beneath the photograph someone has written:
> “ASK MIA WHO SAT AT TABLE SEVEN.”

```item
ask-mia-table-seven
```
:::


:::requires-item{item="table-seven-token"}
:::requires-item{item="table-seven-token"}

Table Seven has changed. You are reasonably certain it was not there before.

You place the token into the indentation.
A drawer opens beneath the table.
Inside:
- a folded Open Wing map
- a broken Sentinel badge
- a handwritten note
> “If the sky starts answering, do not answer back.”
